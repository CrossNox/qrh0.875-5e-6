This note certifies the optimum of an explicitly defined numerical family
using the existing balanced row count and the moment parameter
$\kappa=3/4$. It does not claim optimality of the full method. In
particular, the constraint $l_x+l_y+\ell=1$ is imposed here. Its necessity
in the source construction has not been established. Allowing the total
mass or the moment parameter to vary is a separate problem.
The historical certified zero-free boundary at repository revision
\texttt{c7d46a5} is $0.874958$. The main proof sources now implement a
rational interior geometry with boundary
\[
 B_{\mathrm{rat}}=\frac{4374785349}{5000000000}=0.8749570698.
\]
Its analytic transport and final theorem pass the Lean axiom audit and
Comparator's kernel replay. This rational boundary is distinct
from the exact irrational closure limit derived below.

\section{Geometry and the numerical family}

Let $u,v\in\mathbb R$, $T=u+v$, and set
\[
 l_x=\frac{17}{48}-u,\qquad l_y=\frac{23}{48}-v,\qquad
 \ell=\frac16+T,\qquad h=\frac{13}{16}+2u+v,\qquad b=l_y-l_x.
\]
Then $l_x+l_y+\ell=1$ and $h=1-l_x+\ell$. Neither $u\ge0$ nor
$v\ge0$ is required by the scalar scale inequalities below. The physical
signal and direct low exponent are
\[
 C(s)=\frac{l_x}{2}+s-1+\frac h6
     =s-\frac{11}{16}+\frac{v-u}{6},\qquad
 L=\frac{l_x}{2}+\frac b{12}
     =\frac3{16}-\frac{5u+v}{12}.
\]
For $B=7/8-\eps$, their gap is $C(B)-L=T/4-\eps$.
The signal shift must therefore be included when comparing asymmetric
geometries.

Retain the exact upstream functions
\[
 D(y)=\frac{37+34y}{18},\quad P(y)=\frac{7+18y+8y^2}{9},\quad
 J(\delta,y)=(5/6-\delta)D(y)+\delta P(y),
\]
\[
 R(\delta,y)=1-\delta+
       \frac{(5/6-\delta)\delta P(y)}{2J(\delta,y)},\qquad
 q=\delta(1/2-y),
\]
on $0\le\delta\le3/4$, $0\le y\le1/2$. Here $J\ge35/54>0$.
The prior $\beta_*\le7/8$ supplies the moment parameter $\kappa=3/4$
and the indicated $\delta$ cap. No energy theorem at a smaller
$\kappa$ is used.
Define
\[
 E_0=-\frac1{48}+\frac23\delta+\frac q6-\frac{13}{16}(1-R).
\]
The general source exponent at frequency and row lengths $\omega,d$ is
\begin{align*}
 F={}&l_x(1/2-c)+a+c-1-a l_y-\omega c+dR\\
 &+\omega(a-1/2)+\ell(c-1/2+q),\qquad
 c=17/50,\quad a=(1+\delta)/2.
\end{align*}
At $\omega=d=h$, subtracting $C(B)$ gives exactly
\[
 E_{\eps,u,v}=E_0+\eps+
 u(2R+\delta+q-5/6)+v(R+\delta+q-1/6).
\]
Our family requires $\eps>0$, $T\ge4\eps$, and
$E_{\eps,u,v}\le0$ for every point of this parameter box. These are
closure conditions. Positive low and high savings require strict gaps.

The following sufficient scale conditions define the source-admissible
subfamily considered here. We retain the existing buffers rather than
assuming stronger estimates:
\begin{gather*}
 b>0,\quad \ell>0,\quad l_x-\ell>0,\quad l_y-\ell>0,\quad
 l_x+l_y-2\ell>0,\quad h>0,\quad h<7/8,\\
 0\le T<1/30,\qquad 2l_x-l_y-\ell>0,\qquad
 l_y-\ell-11b/6>0,\\
 \ell>\frac8{39}h,\qquad 5\ell-h>\frac1{48}.
\end{gather*}
The compensated Gram cap $\ell<2l_x-l_y$ absorbs the constants
in $L_{\rm subset}\ll Z^\ell$. It is stronger than the condition
obtained by absorbing the Gram tail directly into its fractional term.
The reflected exponent for a subset of length $d$ is
\[
 -d+\frac18(d-1/6+5T)_+.
\]
It is nonpositive for every $d\ge0$ when $T\le1/30$.
The supply condition $\ell/h>8/39$ retains the source buffer above
$7/37$, which is the maximum inverse selected-prime requirement at
the inverse/plain crossing. It enforces the capacities
$r+2L_A<1$ and $2r+8L_A<3$ after fine-mesh rounding. Plain marked
capacity remains $2m+(9/2)L_A\le1$. Positive individual slot lengths
can be made arbitrarily small, so a fine mesh does not bound their
total length. Distinct lengths retain disjoint prime annuli.

\section{Exact upper bound and attainment}

Put
\[
 s=\sqrt{921},\qquad \delta_*=(49-s)/48,
 \qquad \eps_*=(16s-485)/13224.
\]
At $y=0$ the row count has the rational form
\[
 R(\delta,0)=\frac{185-288\delta+96\delta^2}{185-138\delta}.
\]
The equation $288\delta_*^2-588\delta_*+185=0$ gives
$R(\delta_*,0)=2/3$. At this actual source bin the two shift
coefficients coincide, so
\[
 E_{\eps,u,v}(\delta_*,0)=\frac34\delta_*-\frac7{24}
      +\eps+T(1/2+3\delta_*/2).
\]
The coefficient of $T$ is positive. Thus $T\ge4\eps$ and the high
inequality imply
\[
 \eps\le\frac{7/24-3\delta_*/4}{3+6\delta_*}=\eps_*.
\]
The inequality is strict if the low gap is strict. This argument imposes
no signs on $u$ or $v$, and no other geometry restrictions.

For the matching closure point choose
\[
 u_*=-\frac{5417}{26448}+\frac{3413s}{507471},\qquad
 v_*=\frac{53}{912}-\frac{11s}{5833},\qquad T_*=4\eps_*.
\]
These satisfy stationarity in $\delta$ at $(\delta_*,0)$.
Numerically,
\begin{align*}
 u_*&=-0.000711421104065542371,\\
 v_*&= 0.000883141907390582298,\\
 \eps_*&=0.0000429302008312599817,\\
 7/8-\eps_*&=0.874957069799168740.
\end{align*}
The script \texttt{research/method\_limit\_general.py} reconstructs the
following exact certificate using symbolic rational arithmetic in
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt{921})$. Set
\[
 N=-2J E_{\eps_*,u_*,v_*}=A(y)\delta^2+B(y)\delta+K(y).
\]
It checks the identity
\[
 4A(y)N=(2A(y)\delta+B(y))^2+yH(y),
\]
where $A(y)$ has positive coefficients and
\begin{align*}
 H(y)={}&\frac{241329064550-7669715200s}{203840443809}\\
 &+\left(\frac{596581904575}{815361775236}
          -\frac{397791650s}{22648938201}\right)y\\
 &+\left(-\frac{211863217525}{203840443809}
          +\frac{2926468000s}{67946814603}\right)y^2\\
 &+\left(-\frac{837952475}{1192049379}
          +\frac{275538200s}{10728444411}\right)y^3.
\end{align*}
All four coefficients of $H$ are positive. The script proves these
signs using rational lower and upper enclosures of $s$ obtained from

At $y=0$ it checks
\[
 N(\delta,0)=\left(\frac{884}{1653}
                  -\frac{9172s}{4567239}\right)(\delta-\delta_*)^2.
\]
Consequently the high inequality holds globally, with equality only
at the critical bin. This certifies the exact fixed-mass closure optimum.
Keeping $u_*,v_*$ fixed and taking $\eps=\eps_*-\rho$ gives both
low and high gaps at least $\rho$ for every $\rho>0$.

\]

For the intermediate branch $d\le1/2$, the source estimate
$R\le76/75-(2/3)\delta$ and $q\le\delta/2$ gives
\[
 -529/2400+(25/96)\delta+\eps+
 u(\delta/2-23/150)+v(1/2+\delta-49/150).

At the proposed closure geometry this is linear in $\delta$, so its
two endpoint checks certify the whole interval. The small-row saving is

\section{Conditional extension to independent slot length}

For independent shifts take $\ell=1/6+w$ while retaining
$l_x=17/48-u$, $l_y=23/48-v$, and $h=13/16+u+w$.
Write $T=u+v$. The high perturbation becomes
\[
 \eps+u(R+\delta/2-1/6)+v(1+\delta)/2
       +w(R+\delta/2+q-2/3).
\]
At the same critical bin it reduces to
$\eps+((1+\delta_*)/2)T+\delta_*w$.
If the direct low comparison includes the reflected mass-excess cost
$(w-T)_+/2$, its required gap is
\[
 (T+2w)/12-(w-T)_+/2-\eps\ge0.
\]
Under this explicit assumption the same upper bound follows. For every
$\delta\ge0$, put $k=(1+3\delta)/6$. Then
\begin{align*}
 ((1+\delta)/2)T+\delta w
 ={}&k\bigl(T+2w-6(w-T)_+\bigr)\\
 &+(T-w)/3+(1+3\delta)(w-T)_+.
\end{align*}
The last line is nonnegative. For $w\le T$ it is $(T-w)/3$.
For $w\ge T$ it is $(2/3+3\delta)(w-T)$.
Thus the high cost is at least $(2+6\delta)\eps$, recovering
$\eps\le\eps_*$. The Lean theorem
\begin{verbatim}
bound_independent_slot_boundary_reduction
\end{verbatim}
verifies this conditional arithmetic extension with the actual upstream row count.
It does not prove that the displayed mass-excess cost is forced for
actual detector rows. The separate reflected source audit must supply
that premise before interpreting this as a limit of the larger family.
