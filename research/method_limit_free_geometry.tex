\title{Independent probe lengths and the reflected mass loss}
\author{}
\date{October 8, 2026}
\maketitle

The physical probe and its principal Mellin identities do not require
$x+y+\ell=1$.  This audit identifies the cost of removing that relation
from the current reflected scalar estimate.  It does not prove a lower
bound for the arithmetic row energy.

Write
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 x&=\frac{17}{48}-u,& y&=\frac{23}{48}-v,& \ell&=\frac16+w,\\
 T&=u+v,& M&=x+y=\frac56-T.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The principal scale has $h=1-x+\ell$.  Its exponent
$x/2+s-1+h/6$ therefore changes by $-u/3+w/6$.
The unpenalized LOW exponent changes by $-(5u+v)/12$.
Their relative gain is $(T+2w)/12$.
These identities alone do not justify using the old LOW estimate above
total mass one.

For the reflected scalar estimate, put
\[
 T_d=2H+2A_0+2z_a-1-\ell_0-\theta-N_0-3B_0.
\]
The original proof bounds $T_d\le H-3d+6\eta$ using $M+\ell=1$.
Under the same remaining scalar gates, the unrestricted bound is
\[
 T_d\le H+(M+\ell-1)_+-3d+6\eta.
\]
The same hybrid-saving argument then gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:generalbound}
 E_{\rm ref}\le M-2d+(M+\ell-1)_+
   +\left(\frac{d+1+3\ell-2M}{4}\right)_++40\eta.
\end{equation}
This is proved against the actual upstream definition of
\texttt{reflectedExponent} in
\nolinkurl{lean/MethodLimitFreeGeometry.lean}, theorem
\nolinkurl{bound_free_reflected_exponent_with_mass_penalty}.
The mass-at-most-one corollary recovers the estimate without the added
mass term.  The error parameter $\eta$ here is distinct from the desired
boundary reduction $\varepsilon$.

The added mass term is necessary for this scalar envelope.  Choose
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 O_0&=A_0=N_0=S_0=B_0=\theta=0,& H&=M,\\
 \ell_0&=z_a=\ell,& v&=T_d=2M+\ell-1,\\
 \ell_{\rm cube}&=\ell_{\rm fourth}=0.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The retained gate holds with equality.  If $M\ge1/2$ and $\ell\ge0$,
the nonnegative width gates hold.  Since
\[
 U(v,z_a)=\min\{v,z_a,(v+z_a)/3\}\le z_a,
\]
the actual scalar functional satisfies
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_{\rm ref}&=\max\{M,2M+\ell-1\}+\ell-U(2M+\ell-1,\ell)\\
 &\ge M+(M+\ell-1)_+.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Equality holds whenever $\ell\le2M-1$.
In particular, $M\ge2\ell$ and $M+\ell\ge1$ imply that condition.
The source inequalities $x\le y$ and $\ell\le2x-y$ imply
$M\ge2\ell$.  Thus the witness applies throughout the excess-mass
region of those source scales, even when the other positive part in
\eqref{eq:generalbound} is active.

As an exact counterexample to omitting the mass loss, take
$M=21/25$ and $\ell=17/100$.  Then
$1+3\ell-2M=-17/100$, but the witness has
$E_{\rm ref}=17/20>21/25$.
For the baseline $x,y$ with only $w=1/1000$ added, the Gram cap still
holds with gap $123/2000$.  The principal gain is $1/6000$, while the
reflected energy contributes a LOW exponent cost $1/2000$ after
Cauchy--Schwarz.  The net LOW gain is $-1/3000$.

Consequently, in source geometries with $M\ge1/2$ and $\ell\ge0$,
the empty-subset scalar-envelope gain cannot exceed
\[
 G(T,w)=\frac{T+2w}{12}-\frac{(w-T)_+}{2}.
\]
If the other empty positive part is active, it can only reduce the gain
further.  At the universal endpoint witness with $R=2/3$, $q=\delta/2$
and the height parameter zero, the relative central cost is
\[
 K=\varepsilon+\frac{1+\delta}{2}T+\delta w.
\]
For $\delta\ge0$, imposing $\varepsilon\le G(T,w)$ gives the exact
inequality
\[
 K\ge\varepsilon(3+6\delta)
   +\frac{(T-w)_+}{3}
   +(w-T)_+\left(\frac23+3\delta\right).
\]
No separate sign assumption on $u$ or $v$ is needed.  Below mass one,
the residual cost is $(T-w)/3$.  Above mass one, it is
$(w-T)(2/3+3\delta)$.  Equality in the geometry cost requires $w=T$.
This proves that independent slot mass does not improve the critical
endpoint obstruction within the present scalar-envelope proof.

The transfer of this witness to that ceiling is restricted to source
geometries with $M\ge1/2$ and $\ell\ge0$.
The retained source family supplies the first condition explicitly:
$x\le y$, $h=1-x+\ell$, $h<7/8$ and $\ell>(8/39)h$ imply
\[
 x>1-\frac{31}{39}h>\frac{95}{312},\qquad
 M=x+y\ge2x>\frac{95}{156}>\frac12.
\]
The positive slot lengths supply $\ell\ge0$.
The source-family bridge and the nonnegative dual-width consequence are
proved in \nolinkurl{MethodLimitFreeGeometry.lean}.

The corresponding fixed-mass endpoint ceiling is
\[
 \varepsilon\le\frac{16\sqrt{921}-485}{13224}.
\]
The transfer from the displayed scalar gain assumption to the actual
endpoint obstruction is formalized separately in
\nolinkurl{MethodLimitGeneral.bound_independent_slot_boundary_reduction}.
This is not a limit for all methods.  An improvement would need to
exclude the scalar witness using additional arithmetic information, or
replace the reflected estimate.
